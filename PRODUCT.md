## Users

- **Families planning ahead** (primary): adults roughly 40–65 choosing a burial plot or niche for parents or themselves. They compare zones, size, location, legal standing and price, visit in person before deciding, and decide over weeks.
- **Families with an immediate need** (primary): someone has just died or is about to. They need a phone number and a clear "what happens now" within seconds, often on a phone.
- Secondary: relatives of people already interred (finding a grave, ceremony calendar), overseas Vietnamese viewing remotely.

## Product Purpose

The website of Hoa Viên Nghĩa Trang Bình Dương, a memorial park operated by Công ty CP Đầu tư Xây dựng Chánh Phú Hòa (CPHA). It sells burial rights in planned zones, ash niches at Chùa Thiên Phước, grave construction, and full funeral services. Success: planners book a site visit or request a quote; urgent callers reach the hotline immediately.

## Positioning

A 300 ha park planned since 2006, built around large spiritual landmarks (Linh Hoa Tuệ Đàn, Vĩnh Hằng Đài, Chùa Thiên Phước, Hồn Việt, Vĩnh Hằng Môn) set in forest, with named zones whose standard plot sizes and access roads are published. Fifteen 360° panoramas and a digital park map exist.

## Operating Context

- Open 7h30–17h00 every day including Sundays and holidays; hotline 0818 555 444; website cphaco.vn.
- Address: Khu phố Bông Trang, phường Chánh Phú Hòa, TP. Hồ Chí Minh (formerly Bến Cát, Bình Dương; people still search "Bình Dương").
- Three HCMC branches: 99 Điện Biên Phủ (P. Gia Định), 140C Nguyễn Cư Trinh (P. Cầu Ông Lãnh), 253D Lương Định Của (P. Bình Trưng).
- Annual ceremonies: Cầu Siêu – Cầu An (Thanh Minh), Giỗ Tổ Hùng Vương, Vu Lan, year-end tomb sweeping.
- Free tour bus from the city (photo evidence).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Zones and published standards: Long Phụng Minh Đồ 5,735 m²/plot (near Chùa Thiên Phước); Đại Lộ Vĩnh Hằng 11,63 m²; Linh Hoa Tuệ Đàn 10,87 m²; Song Thân 66 m² (limited); family zone Trúc Lâm Bích Thủy 64–81 m², up to 10 graves.
- Burial rights 50 years or permanent depending on product; 30-day reconsideration if no burial yet; no instalment plans; all construction done by the park's own crew.
- Prices are not published: quote on request. Do not invent prices.

## Product Principles

1. Urgent help is never more than one tap away.
2. Facts before feelings: sizes, places, hours, procedures stated plainly.
3. Show the real place; every photo is of this park.
4. Respect grief: calm, clear, never salesy.
